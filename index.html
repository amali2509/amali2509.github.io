Research

Net-zero pathways for the chemicals and materials industry through life cycle assessment, systems optimization, and technology transition modeling. Advisor: Prof. Bhavik R. Bakshi.

Open-access CMI model

An open-access Chemicals and Materials Industry model that integrates material flow analysis, life cycle assessment, cost modeling, and Julia/JuMP optimization to evaluate industrial decarbonization pathways.

Cost–emissions trade-offs

Pareto frontiers built with the ε-constraint method to evaluate net-zero feasibility, marginal abatement costs, and technology substitutions that reduce both cost and greenhouse gas emissions.

2025–2050 roadmapping

A decarbonization roadmapping framework that incorporates technology readiness level (TRL) evolution, adoption readiness (ARL), process costs, final demand, and resource constraints.

12-scenario TRL/ARL framework

Assesses how technology maturity and adoption barriers affect achievable emissions reductions, transition costs, and the selection of emerging technologies.

Industrial symbiosis

Compares integrated and independently optimized industrial sectors to study shared resource allocation and cross-sector material and energy exchanges for cost-effective decarbonization.

Polyester pathways

Integrates process-level life cycle inventory and cost datasets with optimization workflows to assess polyester production, recycling, and material substitution through sensitivity analysis and scenario comparison.
